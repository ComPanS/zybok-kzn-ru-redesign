# Contemporary reference research

Research completed 4 September 2026. References were used as evidence for information architecture and trust patterns, not as layouts to copy. Claims shown on reference sites remain those sites’ own claims.

| Reference | Useful evidence for this concept |
|---|---|
| [Дентал Клиник, Казань](https://dentalclinic-kazan.ru/) | Calm path from services and prices to clinic/license, doctors, contact and map; one dominant appointment action; visible legal material. |
| [Призматика, Казань](https://prismatica-clinic.ru/) | Doctors function as meaningful decision points; treatment reason is captured before generic clinic promotion; legal information is easy to reach. |
| [СТОМУС](https://stomus.com/) | Prices are available next to the service catalog; insurance and policy information support practical trust. |
| [Городская стоматология, Казань](https://www.gorstom.ru/) | Location/map lowers the local visit barrier; concrete sterilization and equipment evidence is stronger than generic quality claims. |
| [Дентал Белгравия](https://belgraviadent.ru/) | Human authorship, service navigation by patient concern, direct doctor/contact actions, and a dedicated license page. |

## Applied decisions

- Keep the existing blue tooth identity rather than creating an unrelated luxury brand.
- Lead with the clinic’s stated tooth-preservation principle.
- Use real rooms, entrance, staff portraits, license scans, address, schedule and published prices.
- Make phone contact the primary conversion because no verified online booking system was found.
- Present the complete source price catalog before asking for contact details.
- Use a thin blue care-path line as the signature element instead of gradients, 3D teeth or repeated icon cards.
- Avoid copied ratings, awards, guarantees, external messenger channels, equipment claims or other facts not verified on the source site.

## Direction decision

The researched alternative suggested a warm porcelain canvas and serif display face. The implemented direction deliberately stays cooler and more geometric: this better preserves the clinic’s original cobalt square/tooth mark and its practical neighborhood-clinic character. Manrope and Golos Text provide strong Cyrillic coverage and keep the long medical price catalog legible. Warm luxury styling was rejected as a mismatch for the source material.
